The Record

There have also been public officials and political leaders who have faced serious criminal allegations or legal proceedings, yet continued to hold or return to positions of power. Whether those cases ended in conviction, acquittal, dismissal, or remain unresolved, they are part of the public record — and they should not simply disappear from public memory.

What This Platform Does

This platform documents publicly available information about matters of public interest — official statements, investigations, court proceedings, public promises, policy decisions, and their outcomes. It brings verified information together in one place so citizens can understand the bigger picture instead of relying on headlines or rumors.

Hisaab does not decide guilt. It preserves facts, documents public records, and encourages transparency through reliable sources.
