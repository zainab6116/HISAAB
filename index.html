What is a Hisaab Score?

Every profile carries a Hisaab Score — not a judgment of guilt, but a plain summary of the public record: how many offices someone has held, how many investigations and court cases have been publicly reported, and how those cases were resolved. It's a way to see a pattern at a glance, without HISAAB making the judgment for you.
