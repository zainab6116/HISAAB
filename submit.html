That means every submission gets checked against public records, court documents, and credible reporting before it becomes part of the archive — but that verification can't start until someone brings it forward. If you have sourced, documented information, this is where it enters the record.

Before You Submit

Only submit information that is already public, or that you are legally and safely able to share. If you or someone you know is in immediate danger, contact local authorities or a trusted support organization first — this form is for documentation, not emergency response.

We do not publish unverified claims, and we do not disclose who submitted a report. HISAAB makes no judgment of guilt — it documents the public record.
